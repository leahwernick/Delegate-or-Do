Delegate or Do

Twelve tasks land on your desk. Sort each into a tray. About four minutes. Keys 1, 2, 3 work too.

0 / 24 pts
Learning goals
  1. Tell apart tasks where AI helps (quantity, variety, low-stakes summaries, reframing for audiences, second opinions) from tasks where it hurts (learning, high-accuracy work, work where the effort is the point).
  2. Ask three questions before using AI on anything: Can I check the output? What does a mistake cost? Is the effort the point?
  3. Expect the jagged frontier: AI can fail at simple things like counting characters while handling hard things well.
